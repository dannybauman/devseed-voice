# Notebooks made for an audience

**Stop 4 of devseed-voice.** Charted 2026-10-24 from Phase 4 of `docs/BUILD-TRACKER.md`.

## Why this stop exists

A shared notebook lets everyone it is shared with read its sources, even sources they could not open themselves. Before anyone else hears this agent, each notebook it uses must be built for the people who will hear it.

## Done looks like

Every allowlisted notebook says who it is for, and the ones a wider group hears hold only sources that group can already see.
